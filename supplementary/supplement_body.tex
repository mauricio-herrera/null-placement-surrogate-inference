\section{Exact native surrogate algorithm}\label{exact-native-surrogate-algorithm}

For monthly observations \(x_t\), let calendar month be \(m(t)\). Compute observed month-specific \[
\mu_m=\operatorname{mean}\{x_t:m(t)=m\},
\qquad
\sigma_m=\operatorname{sd}\{x_t:m(t)=m\},
\] and standardized values \[
z_t=(x_t-\mu_{m(t)})/\sigma_{m(t)}.
\] Let \[
A_k=|\mathcal F(z-\bar z)_k|
\] be the target Fourier amplitudes.

Groups are either: - calendar month over the full record; or - calendar month \(\times\) fixed year block.

Initialize \(y\) by random permutation within every group. For each iteration:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  standardize \(y\) with the observed \(\mu_m,\sigma_m\);
\item
  retain its Fourier phases and replace amplitudes with \(A_k\);
\item
  inverse transform;
\item
  within each group, rank the projected scores and assign the exact sorted observed \(x_t\) values of that group.
\end{enumerate}

This projection alternates at most 12 times. The final groupwise multiset equals the observed multiset exactly. Therefore any fixed threshold preserves the exact event count within every group.

\section{Exact annual IAAFT algorithm}\label{exact-annual-iaaft-algorithm}

The index-level input is the cubic-detrended annual series. Let the sorted observed values be \(z_{(1)},\ldots,z_{(n)}\), and target Fourier amplitudes \(A_k\). Initialize by random permutation. Each iteration:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  replace current Fourier amplitudes by \(A_k\);
\item
  inverse transform;
\item
  rank-remap to the exact sorted observed detrended values.
\end{enumerate}

The maximum iteration count is 30. Spectral error is normalized mean squared Fourier-amplitude error.

\section{Ferro--Segers implementation}\label{ferrosegers-implementation}

For exceedance times \(s_j\) over the empirical \(q=0.90\) threshold, set \(T_i=s_{i+1}-s_i\). The exact implementation is \[
\hat\theta_{\rm raw}
=
\frac{2(\sum T_i)^2}{n_T\sum T_i^2}
\] when \(\max T_i\le2\), and, with \(S_i=T_i-1\), \[
\hat\theta_{\rm raw}
=
\frac{2(\sum S_i)^2}{n_T\sum S_i(S_i-1)}
\] otherwise. A nonpositive denominator returns one. Frozen tests clip to \([0,1]\).

\section{Generator details}\label{generator-details}

Gaussian AR(1): \[
X_t=\phi X_{t-1}+\sqrt{1-\phi^2}\varepsilon_t.
\]

Periodic-innovation AR(1): \[
X_t=\phi X_{t-1}+\sigma_{m(t)}\varepsilon_t,\qquad
\sigma_m=\exp[0.65\cos(2\pi(m-1)/12)].
\] A 2400-month burn-in was used.

Student-\(t\)-innovation AR(1): \[
X_t=\phi X_{t-1}+\sqrt{3/5}\,T_5,
\] with a 2000-month burn-in. Threshold calibration used a deterministic 300,000-point reference series for each \(\phi\).

Pooled index-only discordance decreases with \(\phi\): \[

\] for \(\phi=0.2,0.5,0.8\). This pooled ordering is composition-dependent. At \(\lambda=4\), the ordering reverses sufficiently that \(\phi=0.2\) becomes the least discordant persistence level. The manuscript therefore does not infer a monotone persistence mechanism from the pooled summary.

\section{Mechanism injections}\label{mechanism-injections}

Persistent regime: \[
L_y=0.85L_{y-1}+\sqrt{1-0.85^2}\xi_y.
\] For each calendar month, exact original values are rank-remapped according to \[
(1-\kappa)R+\kappa L,
\qquad
\kappa\in\{0.2,0.4,0.6,0.8\}.
\]

History feedback: \[
H_t=\sum_{\ell=1}^{24}w_\ell I_{t-\ell},
\qquad
w_\ell\propto e^{-\ell/6}.
\] Within calendar month, target scores are \[
R-\kappa\widetilde H
\] and remapped to exact original values, repeated for four iterations. The grid is \[
\kappa\in\{0.25,0.50,0.75,1.00\}.
\]

\section{Scope of the null comparison}\label{scope-of-the-null-comparison}

The manuscript does not claim that every index-resolution null must show the same behavior. The primary comparison is conditional on: - a detrended annual IAAFT marginal-and-spectrum contract; - the constrained monthly IAAFT-like contract described above; - the fixed cubic detrending rule; - the Ferro--Segers \(q=0.90\) statistic; - the finite sample lengths studied.

Alternative parametric count models, block resampling, or other surrogate constraints remain valid topics for future comparison.
